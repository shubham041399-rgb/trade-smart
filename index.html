Chart Education

Reading a Candlestick
GREEN (Bullish) candle:
Open is at the bottom, Close is at top. Price went UP during this period.

RED (Bearish) candle:
Open is at the top, Close is at bottom. Price went DOWN.

Wicks (Shadows):
Show the extremes — how high/low price went before returning.
Bullish Engulfing
Large green candle engulfs the previous red candle's body completely.
Reversal Signal ↑
Bearish Engulfing
Large red candle engulfs the previous green candle. Sellers took over.
Reversal Signal ↓
Hammer
Long lower wick, small body at top. Buyers rejected lower prices.
Bullish Reversal ↑
Shooting Star
Long upper wick, small body at bottom. Sellers rejected higher prices.
Bearish Reversal ↓
Doji
Open = Close. Equal wicks. Market indecision — trend may change.
Indecision
Morning Star
Red → small body → strong green. 3-candle bullish reversal at bottom.
Strong Bullish ↑
Breakout from Consolidation (Your Setup #1)
Entry: On breakout candle close above resistance + volume
Stop Loss: Below the resistance level (now support)
Target: 2x the height of the consolidation range
Pullback to Support (Your Setup #2)
Entry: Bullish reversal candle at support level in uptrend
Stop Loss: Below the support level / below candle low
Target: Previous high or 2x risk distance
Moving Averages (Start Here)
20 EMA — Short-term trend. Price above = bullish. Below = bearish.
50 EMA — Medium-term trend. Acts as strong support/resistance.
Golden Cross — 20 EMA crosses above 50 EMA = Strong buy signal
Death Cross — 20 EMA crosses below 50 EMA = Strong sell signal
Volume (Always Check This)
📈 Price up + High Volume = Strong move, trustworthy
📈 Price up + Low Volume = Weak move, beware fake breakout
📉 Price down + High Volume = Heavy selling, stay out
📉 Price down + Low Volume = Healthy pullback, possible buy
How to Use Zerodha Kite for Your Setups
Step 1 — Scan Open Kite → Go to Chart → Select stock → Switch to Daily timeframe
Step 2 — Identify Look for either: flat consolidation (Setup 1) OR uptrend with recent pullback (Setup 2)
Step 3 — Validate Check volume. Check 20 EMA direction. Check if there's a clean level.
Step 4 — Plan Write down: Entry price, Stop Loss price, Target price BEFORE you execute.
Step 5 — Risk Check Calculate shares = ₹300 / (Entry - SL). This is your max position size.
Step 6 — Execute In Kite: Buy → set Limit order → immediately set Stop Loss order.
