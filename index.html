How to Read a Candlestick
i

Key Points

Green candle = Close > Open = Buyers won
Red candle = Close < Open = Sellers won
Long wick = price tested that level but was rejected
Small wick = little rejection, strong close
GREEN Candle: Close above Open. Buyers in control.
RED Candle: Close below Open. Sellers in control.

Body size matters: Large body = strong move. Small body = indecision.

Wick meaning: Long upper wick = sellers rejected higher prices. Long lower wick = buyers rejected lower prices.
Bullish Engulfing
Large green candle completely covers previous red candle. Strong reversal signal at bottom.
Reversal ↑
Bearish Engulfing
Large red candle swallows previous green. Strong reversal signal at top.
Reversal ↓
Hammer
Long lower wick, small body at top. Buyers rejected low prices. Appears after downtrend.
Bullish ↑
Shooting Star
Long upper wick, small body at bottom. Sellers rejected higher prices. Appears at top.
Bearish ↓
Doji
Open equals Close. Equal wicks. Complete indecision. Trend change possible.
Indecision
Inverted Hammer
Long upper wick at bottom of downtrend. Buyers tried to push up. Bullish if confirmed next candle.
Potential ↑
Morning Star
3-candle pattern: Red → Small body → Strong Green. Powerful bottom reversal signal.
Strong Bullish ↑
Marubozu (Bull)
Large green candle with no wicks. Pure buying from open to close. Very strong momentum.
Very Bullish ↑
Dragonfly Doji
Long lower wick, open=close at top. Strong rejection of lows. Bullish reversal signal.
Bullish ↑
Gravestone Doji
Long upper wick, open=close at bottom. Strong rejection of highs. Bearish reversal signal.
Bearish ↓
Tweezer Bottom
Two candles with equal lows. First red, second green. Shows buyers defending same level.
Reversal ↑
Hanging Man
Looks like hammer but appears AFTER uptrend. Warning that bulls may be losing control.
Warning ↓
Breakout from Consolidation (Your Setup #1)
i

Key Points

Entry: On candle CLOSING above resistance
SL: Just below the resistance level (now support)
Target: 2× the consolidation range height
Must have: Volume spike on breakout candle
Avoid: Breakout on very low volume = likely fake
Entry: Breakout candle closing above resistance + high volume
SL: Below resistance (now acts as support)
Target: 2× the height of consolidation range
Pullback to Support (Your Setup #2)
i

Key Points

Only trade in direction of main uptrend
Wait for reversal candle AT support — don't buy early
SL: Below the support level or candle low
Target: Previous high minimum
Volume should be lower on pullback, higher on bounce
Entry: Bullish reversal candle forms at support level
SL: Below the support / below candle low
Target: Previous high or 2× risk distance
Head & Shoulders
i

Key Points

3 peaks: left shoulder, head (highest), right shoulder
Neckline: Line connecting the two troughs
Entry: When price BREAKS below neckline
Target: Distance from head to neckline, projected down
One of the most reliable reversal patterns
Top reversal pattern. Left shoulder → Head (highest peak) → Right shoulder → Neckline break = SELL signal. Very reliable after a long uptrend.
Double Top / Bottom
i

Key Points

Double Top: Price hits same high TWICE, then drops — sell
Double Bottom: Price hits same low TWICE, then rises — buy
Entry: After breaking the middle neckline
The two tops/bottoms should be roughly equal
Double Top = bearish reversal (M-shape). Double Bottom = bullish reversal (W-shape). Price tests same level twice and fails both times.
Flag & Pennant
i

Key Points

Flag: Strong move (pole) → small consolidation → breakout
Pennant: Similar but consolidation is triangle-shaped
Both are continuation patterns — trend keeps going
Target: Height of the flagpole projected from breakout
Continuation patterns. Strong move up (flagpole) → brief pause (flag/pennant) → breakout continues in same direction. Entry on breakout from flag.
Cup & Handle
i

Key Points

Rounded bottom (cup) followed by small pullback (handle)
Entry: Breakout above the handle's resistance
Target: Depth of cup projected upward from breakout
Very powerful bullish continuation pattern
Bullish continuation pattern. Price forms rounded bottom (cup) then small pullback (handle) then breaks out. Very powerful setup when found on weekly charts.
Triangle Patterns
i

Key Points

Ascending: Higher lows + flat resistance → bullish breakout
Descending: Lower highs + flat support → bearish breakdown
Symmetrical: Neither side winning → explosive when breaks
Wait for volume confirmation on breakout
3 types: Ascending (bullish), Descending (bearish), Symmetrical (neutral). Price gets compressed then explodes. Trade the breakout direction.
Wedges
i

Key Points

Rising Wedge: Both lines slope up → often BEARISH break
Falling Wedge: Both lines slope down → often BULLISH break
Volume decreases within wedge, spikes on breakout
Counterintuitive — rising wedge usually breaks DOWN
Rising Wedge = bearish (breaks down). Falling Wedge = bullish (breaks up). Both lines converge. Trade the breakout.
Moving Averages
i

Key Points

20 EMA: Short-term. Price above = bullish bias
50 EMA: Medium-term trend direction
200 EMA: Long-term. The most watched MA
Golden Cross: 20 crosses above 50 = BUY signal
Death Cross: 20 crosses below 50 = SELL signal
Price bouncing off EMA = potential entry point
20 EMA — Short-term trend. Dynamic support in uptrends
50 EMA — Medium trend. Stronger support level
200 EMA — Long-term. Above = bull market, below = bear
Golden Cross — 50 EMA crosses above 200 = mega bull signal
Add to Zerodha: Studies → EMA → Period: 20, 50
RSI (Relative Strength Index)
i

Key Points

RSI > 70 = Overbought. Might fall soon. Don't buy.
RSI < 30 = Oversold. Might rise soon. Look for buy.
RSI 50 = Neutral. Above = bullish, below = bearish
RSI Divergence: Price makes new high, RSI doesn't = bearish warning
Use 14-period RSI (default)
Range: 0-100. Most useful: 30 and 70 levels
RSI > 70 — Overbought. Price may reverse down
RSI < 30 — Oversold. Price may bounce up
Best used alongside trend + S&R — not alone

MACD
i

Key Points

MACD Line crosses above Signal = BUY
MACD Line crosses below Signal = SELL
Histogram above zero = bullish momentum
Histogram below zero = bearish momentum
Most useful on daily timeframe for swing trading
MACD Line (12 EMA minus 26 EMA)
Signal Line (9 EMA of MACD)

Bearish crossover — MACD crosses below signal

i

Price up + High volume = Strong, trustworthy move
Price up + Low volume = Weak, possible fake breakout
Price down + High volume = Heavy selling, stay out
Price down + Low volume = Healthy pullback, look to buy
Volume spike = institutional participation

↑ Price + ↓ Volume = Weak, potential fake

i

3 lines: Upper band, Middle (20 EMA), Lower band

Price touching lower band = potentially oversold
Band squeeze = volatility contraction = big move coming
Price walks the upper band = strong uptrend

Band Squeeze = big breakout coming soon

i

Most important: 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%
Draw from swing low to swing high (uptrend)

Use as pullback entry points in setup #2

Weekly Chart — Big picture direction. Never trade against the weekly trend. Check first.

Common Mistakes to Avoid
❌ FOMO — Chasing a stock that already ran. If you missed it, you missed it. Next one.
❌ Revenge Trading — Taking random trades to recover a loss. Destroys capital fast.
❌ Moving SL — Moving stop loss further away when it's about to hit. Accept the loss.
❌ Overtrading — Taking 5-10 trades a day. Quality over quantity. 1-2 good setups a week.
❌ Tips — Trading on WhatsApp/Telegram tips. 99% are wrong. Only your own analysis.
❌ Averaging Down — Buying more of a losing stock. You're doubling a mistake.
Winning Habits
✅ Journal Everything — Every trade logged = faster learning. Pattern recognition.
✅ Plan Before Entry — Entry, SL, target written down before touching buy button.
✅ Respect SL — Always. No exceptions. Small loss beats large loss.
✅ Weekly Review — Every weekend, review all trades. What worked? What didn't?
✅ Take Breaks — After 3 losses in a row, stop for the day. Walk away.
✅ Only Your 2 Setups — If it's not setup #1 or #2, it's not a trade.
