Phase 1 Key Terms

NSENational Stock Exchange — India's largest stock exchange by trading volume
OHLCOpen, High, Low, Close — 4 prices that form every candlestick bar
SupportPrice level where buyers consistently step in, stopping further fall
ResistancePrice level where sellers consistently step in, stopping further rise
VolumeNumber of shares traded. High volume = stronger conviction behind price move
20 EMAExponential Moving Average of last 20 candles. Short-term trend indicator
50 EMAMedium-term trend. Acts as dynamic support in uptrends. Very widely watched.
UptrendSeries of Higher Highs AND Higher Lows — price making higher peaks each time
Swing TradeHolding a stock for 2-10 days to capture one price swing. Not intraday.

May 2025 — Breakout from Consolidation Bullish
🎓 On the chart: Set timeframe to 1D, zoom to April–May 2025
Nifty 50 traded between 24,100–24,600 for 3 weeks. Volume dried up. On breakout day, volume was 2.3× average. Price closed above 24,600 strongly. Measured target: 25,100 (range height added to breakout). This is YOUR Setup #1 playing out on the index.
Entry signal:
Close above 24,600 on high volume
Stop Loss:
Below 24,100 (bottom of consolidation)
Target:
25,100 (measured move)
Feb 2025 — Double Top Reversal Bearish
🎓 On the chart: Set timeframe to 1W, zoom to Nov 2024–Feb 2025
Bank Nifty formed two peaks at 51,800–52,000. Second peak on lower volume = buyer exhaustion. Neckline at 49,200. When price broke below neckline, confirmed the double top. Pattern target: 46,600 (depth of pattern).
Entry signal:
Close below 49,200 neckline
Key lesson:
Second peak on lower volume is the warning sign
Oct 2024 — Ascending Triangle Breakout Bullish
🎓 On the chart: Set timeframe to 1D, zoom to Aug–Oct 2024
Nifty formed rising higher lows while resistance stayed flat near 25,500. Four touches of same resistance level. Buyers becoming more aggressive. Volume spike on breakout day confirmed institutional participation.
Entry signal:
Close above 25,500 with volume spike
Key lesson:
Higher lows = buyers getting more aggressive each time
TCS — March 2025 Pullback to 50 EMA Bullish
🎓 On the chart: Select TCS, set 1D, zoom to Jan–Mar 2025
TCS rallied from ₹3,600 to ₹4,100 in Jan-Feb 2025. Then pulled back to 50 EMA at ₹3,850. Volume was below average during pullback (healthy). Bullish engulfing candle formed AT the 50 EMA on March 12. This is YOUR Setup #2 — textbook pullback to EMA support.
Entry signal:
Bullish engulfing at 50 EMA with volume returning
Stop Loss:
Below the engulfing candle low
INFY — Dec 2024 Ascending Triangle Breakout Bullish
🎓 On the chart: Select INFY, set 1D, zoom to Oct–Dec 2024
Infosys formed ascending triangle from Oct to Dec 2024. Flat resistance at ₹1,940 — tested 4 times. Rising trendline connecting higher lows. Breakout on Dec 18 with 2.1× average volume. Moved toward ₹2,100 target.
Entry signal:
Close above ₹1,940 with volume confirmation
Key lesson:
4 touches of same resistance = strong breakout when it finally breaks
HDFCBANK — Jan 2025 Bearish Flag Bearish
🎓 On the chart: Select HDFCBANK, set 1D, zoom to Dec 2024–Feb 2025
Sharp fall from ₹1,820 to ₹1,640 (flagpole). Then small upward bounce on very low volume (the flag). Many retail traders bought the bounce thinking reversal. Wrong. The flag broke down and continued the decline. Classic bear trap.
Warning signal:
Small bounce on very low volume after sharp fall
Key lesson:
Low volume bounce after big fall = trap, not reversal

Chart Education

How to Read a Candlestick
GREEN: Close above Open. Buyers won.
RED: Close below Open. Sellers won.

Large body = strong move
Long upper wick = sellers rejected higher prices
Long lower wick = buyers rejected lower prices
Zerodha Kite Setup Guide
1
Open Chart: Kite → tap any stock → Chart icon → set timeframe to Daily (D)
2
Add Indicators: Studies → EMA (20, green) → EMA (50, orange) → Volume bars
3
Identify Setup: Flat range → Breakout setup. Pullback in uptrend → Pullback setup.
4
Validate: Volume confirmation? 20 EMA pointing up? Clear level to trade?
5
Plan First: Write Entry, SL, Target BEFORE touching Buy button. No plan = no trade.
6
Risk Check: Shares = ₹200 ÷ (Entry – SL). Max position value: 60% of capital.
7
Execute: Buy (Limit) → IMMEDIATELY set SL-M order. Done.
Multi-Timeframe Analysis
Weekly — Direction only. Never trade against weekly trend.
Daily — PRIMARY chart. Find setups and plan trades here.
4-Hour — Fine-tune entry after finding setup on daily.
1H / 15min — Execution timing only. Too noisy for setup finding.
Process: Weekly (direction) → Daily (setup) → 4H (entry timing)
Mistakes to Avoid
❌ FOMO — Chasing already-moved stocks. Missed = missed.
❌ Revenge Trading — Random trades to recover a loss.
❌ Moving SL — Accept the small loss. Don't move it further.
❌ Overtrading — 1-2 good setups per week beats 10 bad trades daily.
❌ Tips — WhatsApp/Telegram tips will lose you money.
❌ Averaging Down — Buying more of a losing stock = doubling mistake.
Winning Habits
✅ Journal Everything — Every trade logged = faster improvement.
✅ Plan Before Entry — Entry, SL, target BEFORE Buy button.
✅ Respect SL — Always. Every time. Small loss beats large loss.
✅ Weekly Review — Review all trades every weekend.
✅ Take Breaks — 3 losses in a row = stop for the day.
✅ Your 2 Setups — Not setup #1 or #2 = not a trade.
