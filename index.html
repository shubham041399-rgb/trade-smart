Phase 1 Key Terms — Detailed Hinglish Summary

NSE & BSEYeh India ke do main stock exchanges hain jahan shares kharide aur beche jaate hain. NSE (National Stock Exchange) sabse bada hai trading volume ke hisaab se — isliye Zerodha pe zyaadatar NSE use karo. Liquidity zyada hogi toh entry-exit fast aur tighter spread ke saath hogi.
OHLC — Candlestick StructureOpen (jahan se candle shuru hui), High (din ka sabse upar ka point), Low (din ka sabse neeche ka point), Close (jahan candle band hui). Body batati hai ki buyers ya sellers mein kaun jeeta. Wicks (shadows) dikhate hain ki price kitna far gayi lekin wapas pull hui — yeh REJECTION zones hain, bahut important.
Support LevelWoh floor zone jahan price aate hi buyers active ho jaate hain aur girna rok dete hain. Agar price 3-4 baar ek hi level se bounce karti rahe — woh strong support hai. Swing traders ke liye yeh entry zones hote hain. Jitni zyada bounces, utna strong support.
Resistance LevelMarket ki ceiling — jahan heavy selling pressure hota hai aur price ko upar badhne se rok deta hai. Jab price yahan tak pahunche aur sellers haavi ho jaayein toh price wapas neeche jaati hai. Resistance break hone ke liye HIGH VOLUME chahiye — warna fake breakout ho sakta hai.
Volume — Trading Ka FuelVolume matlab kitne shares us time period mein trade hue. Yeh price move ka CONFIRMATION deta hai. Price upar gayi + volume high = genuine buying, institutions aa rahe hain. Price upar gayi + volume low = weak move, fake out ho sakta hai. Volume hamesha price ke saath analyze karo, akele nahi.
20 EMA & 50 EMAExponential Moving Average — last 20 ya 50 candles ka weighted average. Yeh DYNAMIC support aur resistance ka kaam karta hai. 20 EMA short-term momentum batata hai, 50 EMA medium-term trend dikhata hai. Agar price dono ke upar hai (Price > 20 EMA > 50 EMA) toh BULLISH ALIGNMENT — yahi mein swing buy setups dhundo.
Uptrend vs DowntrendUptrend = Higher Highs AND Higher Lows — har peak pichle se upar, har trough pichle se upar. Downtrend = Lower Highs AND Lower Lows. Swing traders sirf uptrend mein long (buy) trade lete hain. Trend ke against trade lena beginners ke liye sabse bada risk hai.
Candlestick PatternsHammer: long lower wick + choti body = buyers ne sellers ko reject kiya. Doji: open ≈ close = market confused, indecision. Bullish Engulfing: badi green candle pichli red ko dhak le = strong buyers aa gaye. Shooting Star: long upper wick + choti body at top = buyers reject hue, reversal possible. In patterns ka matlab context ke saath samjho — akele mat dekho.
Swing Trade2 se 10 din tak stock hold karna ek price swing capture karne ke liye. Na intraday (same day), na investor (months/years). Aapka timeframe daily chart hai. Entry plan karo, SL lagao, target set karo — phir plan follow karo. Emotional decisions = losses.

Phase 2 Key Terms — Detailed Hinglish Summary

Consolidation — Sideways RangeJab market ek tight range mein sideways chalti hai — na upar ja rahi, na neeche. Buyers aur sellers dono ek hi zone mein fight kar rahe hote hain. Sochlo jaise ek coil compress ho rahi ho. Is dauran volume ghatna chahiye — yeh energy store hone ki sign hai. Jitni lambi consolidation, utna bada aur sharp breakout ho sakta hai.
Breakout Setup (Tumhara Setup #1)Jab price resistance zone ke UPAR ek strong candle se CLOSE ho jaaye aur us candle ka volume normal se 1.5x-2x zyada ho — yeh valid breakout hai. Entry: breakout candle close hone ke baad ya agle candle ke open pe. SL: resistance ke thoda neeche (ab woh support ban gaya). Target: consolidation ki height jitna upar. Kabhi bhi running candle mein seedha jump mat karo.
Fake Breakout — Sabse Bada TrapPrice resistance ke upar jaati hai lekin wapas range mein aa jaati hai — yeh FAKE BREAKOUT hai. Isse bachne ka rule: breakout candle ka CLOSE resistance ke upar hona chahiye, sirf wick cross karna kaafi nahi. Aur volume LOW ho toh almost 80% chance hai ki fake hai. Wapas range mein close = turant exit.
Pullback Setup (Tumhara Setup #2)Uptrend mein price 3-5 din ke liye neeche aati hai LOW VOLUME ke saath (panic nahi, sirf correction). Jab yeh kisi strong support pe aake ruke — chahe 20 EMA ho, trendline ho, ya S&R — aur wahan REVERSAL CANDLE bane (Hammer ya Bullish Engulfing) — yahi tumhara entry point hai. Yeh GIFT hai — better price pe entry milti hai strong trend mein.
Stop Loss — Non-Negotiable RuleTrade enter karne se PEHLE SL decide karo, baad mein nahi. SL ek technical level pe lagao — support ke neeche ya reversal candle ke low ke neeche. Kabhi bhi SL ko neeche mat khiskaao kyunki price wahan pahunch gayi — yeh emotional trap hai. "SL hit hua aur price wapas gayi" — yeh normal hai, part of the game hai.
1:2 Risk:Reward RatioAgar tum ₹100 risk kar rahe ho toh target minimum ₹200 ka hona chahiye. Mathematically: agar tum 50% trades mein bhi galat hote ho, phir bhi net profitable rahoge 1:2 R:R ke saath (5 wins × ₹200 = ₹1000, 5 losses × ₹100 = ₹500, net = +₹500). Is ratio se chhota koi trade mat lo.
Entry Timing — Volume ConfirmationEntry sirf tabhi lo jab DO cheezein ek saath milein: (1) Price sahi level pe ho (support ya breakout zone) aur (2) Volume ka confirmation ho. Volume ke bina price move = weak, unreliable. Volume spike ke saath price move = institutional participation = trust karo.

Phase 3 Key Terms — Detailed Hinglish Summary

Position Sizing — Most Critical SkillFormula: Shares = Max Risk Amount ÷ SL Distance. Example: Capital ₹10,000, Risk 2% = ₹200 max risk. SL distance ₹10. Shares = ₹200 ÷ ₹10 = 20 shares. Lekin 20 × stock price bhi dekho — agar total position tumhari capital se zyada hai toh shares kam karo. Dono constraints follow karo: risk limit AND capital limit.
The 2% Rule — Account BachaoApni total capital ka maximum 2% hi ek trade mein risk karo. ₹10,000 capital pe = max ₹200 per trade loss. Yeh rule consecutive losing streaks mein account protect karta hai. 10 consecutive losses bhi 2% rule pe = capital ka sirf 18% khatam — account wapas recover ho sakta hai. Bina is rule ke, 5 bade losses mein account 50% se zyada khatam ho sakta hai.
1:2 Risk:Reward — Math Tumhare Saath HaiHar trade mein target risk ka 2x hona chahiye. Proof: 10 trades mein 5 sahi, 5 galat. 5 wins × ₹200 = ₹1,000. 5 losses × ₹100 = ₹500. NET PROFIT = ₹500 — aur tum sirf 50% accurate the! 1:2 R:R ke bina 60-70% accuracy chahiye profitable rehne ke liye. Is ratio se chhota koi trade mat lo, chahe kitna bhi achha setup lage.
Stop Loss Placement — Technical, Not RandomSL hamesha ek TECHNICAL level pe lagao — support ke neeche, reversal candle ke low ke neeche, ya EMA ke neeche. Kabhi round number pe mat lagao (jaise exactly ₹500). Kabhi "mujhe itna loss nahi chahiye" ke hisaab se mat lagao. SL ek safety net hai — tight ho lekin logical level pe ho. SL hit hona trading ka normal hissa hai, failure nahi.
Trailing Stop Loss — Profit Lock KaroJab trade tumhare favor mein move kare aur target ke paas pahunche, SL ko upar le jaao break-even pe ya profit zone mein. Example: Buy ₹100, SL ₹94. Price ₹112 pe. Ab SL ko ₹108 pe le jaao. Agar price aur upar jaye — winner mein raho. Agar reverse ho — minimum profit ke saath niklo. Yeh "free trade" concept hai — downside eliminate ho jaata hai.
Brokerage Impact on Small CapitalZerodha: ₹20 per executed order (buy aur sell = ₹40 total) + STT (~0.1%) + GST + other charges. ₹10,000 trade pe total cost roughly ₹55-70. Iska matlab ₹10,000 pe ₹2 profit = LOSS after charges. Isliye 1:2 R:R aur minimum ₹150-200+ target ka hona zaroori hai. Chhote targets = brokerage kha jaata hai sab.

Chart Education

How to Read a Candlestick
GREEN: Close above Open. Buyers won.
RED: Close below Open. Sellers won.

Large body = strong move
Long upper wick = sellers rejected higher prices
Long lower wick = buyers rejected lower prices
Continuation Patterns
🚩 Flag Pattern
Strong move ke baad chhota consolidation — flag shape banta hai. Breakout original direction mein hota hai.
Entry: Flag ka upper boundary break hone pe.
SL: Flag ke low ke neeche.
Target: Flag pole ki height = target.
📐 Pennant
Flag jaisa hai lekin consolidation triangular hota hai. Volume breakout pe badhna chahiye confirm ke liye.
Entry: Triangle breakout pe.
SL: Pennant ke low ke neeche.
Target: Pole height add karo.
☕ Cup & Handle
U-shape banta hai phir chhota handle. Weekly chart pe zyada reliable. Breakout = strong bullish signal.
Entry: Handle ka resistance break hone pe.
SL: Handle ke low ke neeche.
Target: Cup depth = target.
📊 Ascending Triangle
Flat top resistance + rising lows. Buyers stronger hote ja rahe hain. Breakout mostly upar hota hai.
Entry: Flat resistance break pe.
SL: Last swing low ke neeche.
Target: Triangle height add karo.
Reversal Patterns
🏔️ Head & Shoulders
Bearish reversal pattern. Teen peaks — beech wala sabse upar (head). Neckline break = strong sell signal.
Entry: Neckline break ke baad retest pe.
SL: Right shoulder ke upar.
Target: Head to neckline distance = target.
⛰️⛰️ Double Top
Do baar same level pe rejection — bearish. Neckline (support) break confirm karta hai reversal.
Entry: Neckline break pe.
SL: Tops ke upar.
Target: Tops to neckline distance = target.
🏔️ Double Bottom
Do baar same support pe bounce — bullish reversal. W-shape banta hai.
Entry: Neckline (resistance) break pe.
SL: Bottoms ke neeche.
Target: Bottoms to neckline distance = target.
📉 Descending Triangle
Flat bottom support + falling highs. Sellers zyada strong. Breakout mostly neeche hota hai.
Entry: Flat support break pe (short).
SL: Last swing high ke upar.
Target: Triangle height minus karo.
Trend Indicators
📈 20 EMA
Short-term trend indicator. Price EMA ke upar = bullish bias. Swing traders ke liye sabse important EMA.
Use: Price 20 EMA pe pullback kare aur bounce kare → entry signal.
Zerodha Kite: Studies → EMA → Period 20
📈 50 EMA
Medium-term trend. 20 EMA 50 EMA ke upar cross kare → strong bullish signal (Golden Cross).
Use: Trend direction confirm karne ke liye. 50 EMA = major support.
Zerodha Kite: Studies → EMA → Period 50
〰️ RSI (14)
Momentum indicator — 0 to 100. Above 70 = overbought (sell zone). Below 30 = oversold (buy zone).
Use: RSI divergence sabse powerful signal — price naya high banaye RSI na banaye = weakness.
Zerodha Kite: Studies → RSI → Period 14
⚡ MACD
Moving Average Convergence Divergence. MACD line signal line cross kare = entry/exit signal.
Use: Bullish crossover (MACD signal ke upar aaye) + price uptrend mein = buy setup.
Settings: 12, 26, 9 (default)
🎯 Bollinger Bands
3 lines — middle (20 SMA) + upper/lower bands (2 std dev). Price bands ke bahar = extreme move.
Use: Band squeeze = breakout aane wala. Price lower band touch kare uptrend mein = buy.
Settings: 20 period, 2 std dev
📊 Volume
Sabse underrated indicator. High volume + price move = genuine. Low volume + price move = fake breakout.
Rule: Hamesha price ke saath volume confirm karo. Volume average se 1.5x upar hona chahiye breakout pe.
NSE minimum: 5 lakh shares/day

Multi-Timeframe Analysis
Weekly — Direction only. Never trade against weekly trend.
Daily — PRIMARY chart. Find setups and plan trades here.
4-Hour — Fine-tune entry after finding setup on daily.
1H / 15min — Execution timing only. Too noisy for setup finding.
Process: Weekly (direction) → Daily (setup) → 4H (entry timing)
Mistakes to Avoid
❌ FOMO — Chasing already-moved stocks. Missed = missed.
❌ Revenge Trading — Random trades to recover a loss.
❌ Moving SL — Accept the small loss. Don't move it further.
❌ Overtrading — 1-2 good setups per week beats 10 bad trades daily.
❌ Tips — WhatsApp/Telegram tips will lose you money.
❌ Averaging Down — Buying more of a losing stock = doubling mistake.
Winning Habits
✅ Journal Everything — Every trade logged = faster improvement.
✅ Plan Before Entry — Entry, SL, target BEFORE Buy button.
✅ Respect SL — Always. Every time. Small loss beats large loss.
✅ Weekly Review — Review all trades every weekend.
✅ Take Breaks — 3 losses in a row = stop for the day.
✅ Your 2 Setups — Not setup #1 or #2 = not a trade.
