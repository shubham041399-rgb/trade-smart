How to Read a Candlestick
GREEN: Close above Open. Buyers won.
RED: Close below Open. Sellers won.

Large body = strong move
Long upper wick = sellers rejected higher prices
Long lower wick = buyers rejected lower prices
🕯️ Basic Candles
1. Marubozu (Bullish) 🟩
Kaisi dikhti hai: Badi green body, koi wick nahi ya bahut chhoti.
Matlab: Buyers ne pure din control rakha. Open se close tak lagatar buying.
Signal: Strong bullish momentum. Agli candle bhi green hone ki zyada chance.
Kab use karein: Breakout ke saath aaye toh entry valid. Volume high hona chahiye.
2. Marubozu (Bearish) 🟥
Kaisi dikhti hai: Badi red body, koi wick nahi ya bahut chhoti.
Matlab: Sellers ne pure din control rakha. Open se close tak lagatar selling.
Signal: Strong bearish momentum. Downtrend continue ho sakta hai.
Kab use karein: Support break ke saath aaye toh short setup valid.
3. Hammer 🔨
Kaisi dikhti hai: Chhoti body upar, bahut lamba lower wick (body ka 2x+), koi upper wick nahi.
Matlab: Bears ne price neeche dhakela, lekin bulls ne wapas upar kheencha. Rejection of lows.
Signal: Downtrend ke baad aaye toh bullish reversal signal.
Kab use karein: Support level pe hammer = strong buy signal. Next candle green confirm karo.
4. Hanging Man 🪆
Kaisi dikhti hai: Hammer jaisi dikhti hai lekin uptrend ke baad aati hai.
Matlab: Bulls ka control kamzor pad raha hai. Bears zyada aggressive ho rahe hain.
Signal: Uptrend ke top pe = bearish reversal warning.
Kab use karein: Confirmation zaroor lo — agli candle red honi chahiye confirm ke liye.
5. Shooting Star ⭐
Kaisi dikhti hai: Chhoti body neeche, bahut lamba upper wick, koi lower wick nahi.
Matlab: Bulls ne price upar dhakela lekin bears ne wapas neeche kheeencha. Rejection of highs.
Signal: Uptrend ke baad = strong bearish reversal signal.
Kab use karein: Resistance level pe shooting star = sell/short setup. Volume confirm karo.
6. Inverted Hammer 🔁
Kaisi dikhti hai: Chhoti body neeche, lamba upper wick. Shooting star jaisi but downtrend mein.
Matlab: Bears ka pressure kam ho raha hai. Bulls price upar le gaye lekin close nahi kar paye.
Signal: Downtrend ke baad = potential bullish reversal.
Kab use karein: Confirmation candle (green) ka wait karo. Support pe zyada reliable.
🔄 Doji Candles — Indecision
7. Standard Doji ➕
Kaisi dikhti hai: Open aur close almost same — cross ya plus sign jaise dikhta hai.
Matlab: Market mein indecision hai — buyers aur sellers equal hain.
Signal: Trend reversal ka warning. Akele strong nahi — context dekhna zaroori.
Kab use karein: Key level pe doji = caution signal. Agli candle direction batayegi.
8. Long-Legged Doji 🕊️
Kaisi dikhti hai: Doji lekin dono wicks bahut lambi — upar aur neeche dono taraf.
Matlab: Extreme volatility — buyers aur sellers dono ne try kiya lekin koi nahi jeeta.
Signal: Big move aane wala hai — kisi bhi direction mein.
Kab use karein: Breakout ke baad direction follow karo. Side mein raho jab tak clear na ho.
9. Dragonfly Doji 🐉
Kaisi dikhti hai: Open = Close = High (body aur upper wick nahi), sirf lamba lower wick.
Matlab: Bears ne price bahut neeche dhakela lekin bulls ne puri wapasi ki — T shape.
Signal: Downtrend mein = strong bullish reversal signal.
Kab use karein: Support level pe dragonfly doji = high probability buy. Volume dekhna zaroori.
10. Gravestone Doji ⚰️
Kaisi dikhti hai: Open = Close = Low, sirf lamba upper wick — inverted T shape.
Matlab: Bulls ne price bahut upar uthaya lekin bears ne puri wapasi ki.
Signal: Uptrend mein = strong bearish reversal signal.
Kab use karein: Resistance pe gravestone = sell signal. Confirmation candle ka wait karo.
🕯️🕯️ Two-Candle Patterns
11. Bullish Engulfing 🟩🟩
Kaisi dikhti hai: Pehli red candle, doosri green candle jo pehli ko puri tarah engulf kare.
Matlab: Bears ne control khoya, bulls ne puri tarah le liya. Strong momentum shift.
Signal: Downtrend ke baad = powerful bullish reversal. Top 3 patterns mein se ek.
Kab use karein: Support pe engulfing = high confidence buy. Volume high hona chahiye.
12. Bearish Engulfing 🟥🟥
Kaisi dikhti hai: Pehli green candle, doosri red candle jo pehli ko puri tarah engulf kare.
Matlab: Bulls ka control gaya, bears ne complete control liya. Strong selling pressure.
Signal: Uptrend ke baad = powerful bearish reversal signal.
Kab use karein: Resistance pe bearish engulfing = exit longs / enter shorts.
13. Piercing Line 📌
Kaisi dikhti hai: Badi red candle, phir green candle jo red candle ke 50% se zyada upar close kare.
Matlab: Bears ka momentum ruk gaya. Bulls ne recovery shuru ki.
Signal: Downtrend mein = moderate bullish reversal. Engulfing se thoda weak.
Kab use karein: Support ke paas aur volume ke saath reliable. Confirmation lo.
14. Dark Cloud Cover ☁️
Kaisi dikhti hai: Badi green candle, phir red candle jo green candle ke 50% se zyada neeche close kare.
Matlab: Bulls thak gaye. Bears ne control lena shuru kiya.
Signal: Uptrend mein = bearish reversal signal. Piercing line ka opposite.
Kab use karein: Resistance ke paas = exit signal. Agli candle se confirm karo.
15. Tweezer Bottom 🔧🔧
Kaisi dikhti hai: Do candles jinke lows exactly same hon — ek red, ek green.
Matlab: Same support level pe do baar rejection — bears fail hue.
Signal: Downtrend ke baad = double support confirmation, bullish reversal.
Kab use karein: Key support pe tweezer bottom = strong buy zone. SL lows ke neeche rakho.
16. Tweezer Top 🔧🔧
Kaisi dikhti hai: Do candles jinke highs exactly same hon — ek green, ek red.
Matlab: Same resistance level pe do baar rejection — bulls fail hue.
Signal: Uptrend ke baad = double resistance confirmation, bearish reversal.
Kab use karein: Key resistance pe tweezer top = exit zone. SL highs ke upar rakho.
🕯️🕯️🕯️ Three-Candle Patterns
17. Morning Star 🌅
Kaisi dikhti hai: Badi red → Chhoti candle (gap down) → Badi green (gap up).
Matlab: Bears ka power khatam, transition ho raha hai, bulls aaye. Trilogy of reversal.
Signal: Downtrend ke baad = strongest bullish reversal patterns mein se ek.
Kab use karein: Support pe morning star = high confidence entry. Risk:Reward bahut achha hota hai.
18. Evening Star 🌆
Kaisi dikhti hai: Badi green → Chhoti candle (gap up) → Badi red (gap down).
Matlab: Bulls ka power khatam, bears wapas aye. Morning star ka opposite.
Signal: Uptrend ke baad = strongest bearish reversal patterns mein se ek.
Kab use karein: Resistance pe evening star = exit longs immediately.
19. Three White Soldiers 💂💂💂
Kaisi dikhti hai: Teen lagatar badi green candles, har ek pichli se upar close kare.
Matlab: Strong sustained buying — bulls completely in control teen dino tak.
Signal: Downtrend ke baad = very strong bullish reversal / continuation.
Kab use karein: Pattern ke baad pullback ka wait karo entry ke liye. Direct entry risky ho sakti hai.
20. Three Black Crows 🐦🐦🐦
Kaisi dikhti hai: Teen lagatar badi red candles, har ek pichli se neeche close kare.
Matlab: Strong sustained selling — bears completely in control teen dino tak.
Signal: Uptrend ke baad = very strong bearish reversal signal.
Kab use karein: Longs exit karo. Short entry ke liye bounce ka wait karo.
21. Morning Doji Star 🌅✨
Kaisi dikhti hai: Badi red → Doji (indecision) → Badi green.
Matlab: Morning star se bhi zyada powerful — beech mein doji perfect indecision dikhata hai.
Signal: Downtrend reversal — morning star se stronger version.
Kab use karein: Support pe = very high confidence buy setup.
22. Evening Doji Star 🌆✨
Kaisi dikhti hai: Badi green → Doji → Badi red.
Matlab: Evening star se zyada powerful — doji perfect uncertainty dikhata hai top pe.
Signal: Uptrend reversal — evening star se stronger.
Kab use karein: Resistance pe = strong sell/exit signal.
23. Three Inside Up 📈
Kaisi dikhti hai: Badi red → Chhoti green (harami inside red) → Badi green confirm.
Matlab: Bearish harami ke baad confirmation — bulls ne control le liya.
Signal: Confirmed bullish reversal — teen candle confirmation.
Kab use karein: Teen candle complete hone ke baad entry — low risk setup.
24. Three Inside Down 📉
Kaisi dikhti hai: Badi green → Chhoti red (harami inside green) → Badi red confirm.
Matlab: Bullish harami ke baad bears ne confirm kiya — selling pressure bada.
Signal: Confirmed bearish reversal.
Kab use karein: Resistance pe = exit signal after confirmation.
⚡ Special & Advanced Patterns
25. Harami (Bullish) 🤰
Kaisi dikhti hai: Badi red candle, phir chhoti green candle jo puri tarah andar ho (harami = pregnant).
Matlab: Bears ka momentum ruk gaya. Chhoti body = indecision aaya.
Signal: Potential bullish reversal — confirmation zaroori.
Kab use karein: Support pe harami + next green candle = entry signal.
26. Harami (Bearish) 🤰
Kaisi dikhti hai: Badi green candle, phir chhoti red candle jo andar ho.
Matlab: Bulls ka momentum ruka. Market soch raha hai — direction change ho sakta hai.
Signal: Potential bearish reversal.
Kab use karein: Resistance pe harami + next red candle = exit/short signal.
27. Spinning Top (Bullish context) 🌀
Kaisi dikhti hai: Chhoti body (any color), dono taraf equal wicks.
Matlab: Market mein balance hai — na buyers na sellers haave. Pure indecision.
Signal: Downtrend mein = bulls wapas aa rahe hain, reversal possible.
Kab use karein: Akele trade mat lo. Strong candle ke saath confirmation lo.
28. Kicker Pattern 🦵
Kaisi dikhti hai: Red candle ke baad gap up karke green candle — ya green ke baad gap down red.
Matlab: Sudden complete sentiment change — news ya event driven. Bahut rare.
Signal: Sabse powerful reversal pattern — price rarely wapas aati hai.
Kab use karein: Gap direction mein strong entry. SL gap ke dono candles ke extreme ke neeche/upar.
29. Rising Three Methods 📊
Kaisi dikhti hai: Badi green → teen chhoti red (consolidation) → badi green breakout.
Matlab: Uptrend mein brief rest — bears try kiya lekin bulls ne control wapas liya.
Signal: Strong bullish continuation pattern.
Kab use karein: Paanchvi candle (green breakout) pe entry. SL chhoti candles ke low ke neeche.
30. Falling Three Methods 📉
Kaisi dikhti hai: Badi red → teen chhoti green (bounce) → badi red breakdown.
Matlab: Downtrend mein brief bounce — bulls try kiya lekin bears wapas aaye.
Signal: Strong bearish continuation pattern.
Kab use karein: Paanchvi candle (red breakdown) pe short. SL chhoti candles ke high ke upar.
31. Bullish Belt Hold 🥋
Kaisi dikhti hai: Gap down open, phir seedha upar — koi lower wick, long green body.
Matlab: Bears ne shuru kiya lekin bulls ne turant control liya — aggressive buying.
Signal: Downtrend mein = strong bullish reversal.
Kab use karein: Gap down ke baad belt hold = institutional buying signal.
32. Bearish Belt Hold 🥋
Kaisi dikhti hai: Gap up open, phir seedha neeche — koi upper wick, long red body.
Matlab: Bulls ne shuru kiya lekin bears ne turant control liya — aggressive selling.
Signal: Uptrend mein = strong bearish reversal.
Kab use karein: Gap up ke baad belt hold = institutional selling signal.
33. High Wave Candle 🌊
Kaisi dikhti hai: Bahut chhoti body, dono taraf bahut lambi wicks — long-legged doji jaisi but body hai.
Matlab: Extreme volatility, complete chaos — buyers aur sellers dono strongly active.
Signal: Major uncertainty — big move aane se pehle ki calm.
Kab use karein: Trade mat lo jab tak direction clear na ho. Breakout side follow karo.
34. On Neck Line 📏
Kaisi dikhti hai: Badi red candle, phir chhoti green candle jo previous red ke close pe close ho.
Matlab: Bulls ne try kiya wapas aane ka lekin fail — bearish pressure continue hai.
Signal: Bearish continuation — sell the bounce.
Kab use karein: Downtrend mein bounce pe short entry. SL green candle ke high ke upar.
35. Abandoned Baby (Bull) 👶
Kaisi dikhti hai: Badi red → Doji (gap down, isolated) → Badi green (gap up).
Matlab: Doji completely isolated — abandoned. Bears aur bulls dono ne chhoda beech mein.
Signal: Rare lekin extremely powerful bullish reversal.
Kab use karein: Gap-up green candle pe entry — very high probability setup.

Continuation Patterns
🚩 Flag Pattern
Strong move ke baad chhota consolidation — flag shape banta hai. Breakout original direction mein hota hai.
Entry: Flag ka upper boundary break hone pe.
SL: Flag ke low ke neeche.
Target: Flag pole ki height = target.
📐 Pennant
Flag jaisa hai lekin consolidation triangular hota hai. Volume breakout pe badhna chahiye confirm ke liye.
Entry: Triangle breakout pe.
SL: Pennant ke low ke neeche.
Target: Pole height add karo.
☕ Cup & Handle
U-shape banta hai phir chhota handle. Weekly chart pe zyada reliable. Breakout = strong bullish signal.
Entry: Handle ka resistance break hone pe.
SL: Handle ke low ke neeche.
Target: Cup depth = target.
📊 Ascending Triangle
Flat top resistance + rising lows. Buyers stronger hote ja rahe hain. Breakout mostly upar hota hai.
Entry: Flat resistance break pe.
SL: Last swing low ke neeche.
Target: Triangle height add karo.
Reversal Patterns
🏔️ Head & Shoulders
Bearish reversal pattern. Teen peaks — beech wala sabse upar (head). Neckline break = strong sell signal.
Entry: Neckline break ke baad retest pe.
SL: Right shoulder ke upar.
Target: Head to neckline distance = target.
⛰️⛰️ Double Top
Do baar same level pe rejection — bearish. Neckline (support) break confirm karta hai reversal.
Entry: Neckline break pe.
SL: Tops ke upar.
Target: Tops to neckline distance = target.
🏔️ Double Bottom
Do baar same support pe bounce — bullish reversal. W-shape banta hai.
Entry: Neckline (resistance) break pe.
SL: Bottoms ke neeche.
Target: Bottoms to neckline distance = target.
📉 Descending Triangle
Flat bottom support + falling highs. Sellers zyada strong. Breakout mostly neeche hota hai.
Entry: Flat support break pe (short).
SL: Last swing high ke upar.
Target: Triangle height minus karo.
Trend Indicators
📈 20 EMA
Short-term trend indicator. Price EMA ke upar = bullish bias. Swing traders ke liye sabse important EMA.
Use: Price 20 EMA pe pullback kare aur bounce kare → entry signal.
Zerodha Kite: Studies → EMA → Period 20
📈 50 EMA
Medium-term trend. 20 EMA 50 EMA ke upar cross kare → strong bullish signal (Golden Cross).
Use: Trend direction confirm karne ke liye. 50 EMA = major support.
Zerodha Kite: Studies → EMA → Period 50
〰️ RSI (14)
Momentum indicator — 0 to 100. Above 70 = overbought (sell zone). Below 30 = oversold (buy zone).
Use: RSI divergence sabse powerful signal — price naya high banaye RSI na banaye = weakness.
Zerodha Kite: Studies → RSI → Period 14
⚡ MACD
Moving Average Convergence Divergence. MACD line signal line cross kare = entry/exit signal.
Use: Bullish crossover (MACD signal ke upar aaye) + price uptrend mein = buy setup.
Settings: 12, 26, 9 (default)
🎯 Bollinger Bands
3 lines — middle (20 SMA) + upper/lower bands (2 std dev). Price bands ke bahar = extreme move.
Use: Band squeeze = breakout aane wala. Price lower band touch kare uptrend mein = buy.
Settings: 20 period, 2 std dev
📊 Volume
Sabse underrated indicator. High volume + price move = genuine. Low volume + price move = fake breakout.
Rule: Hamesha price ke saath volume confirm karo. Volume average se 1.5x upar hona chahiye breakout pe.
NSE minimum: 5 lakh shares/day
Zerodha Kite Setup Guide
1
Open Chart: Kite → tap any stock → Chart icon → set timeframe to Daily (D)
2
Add Indicators: Studies → EMA (20, green) → EMA (50, orange) → Volume bars
3
Identify Setup: Flat range → Breakout setup. Pullback in uptrend → Pullback setup.
4
Validate: Volume confirmation? 20 EMA pointing up? Clear level to trade?
5
Plan First: Write Entry, SL, Target BEFORE touching Buy button. No plan = no trade.
6
Risk Check: Shares = ₹200 ÷ (Entry – SL). Max position value: 60% of capital.
7
Execute: Buy (Limit) → IMMEDIATELY set SL-M order. Done.
Multi-Timeframe Analysis
Weekly — Direction only. Never trade against weekly trend.
Daily — PRIMARY chart. Find setups and plan trades here.
4-Hour — Fine-tune entry after finding setup on daily.
1H / 15min — Execution timing only. Too noisy for setup finding.
Process: Weekly (direction) → Daily (setup) → 4H (entry timing)
Mistakes to Avoid
❌ FOMO — Chasing already-moved stocks. Missed = missed.
❌ Revenge Trading — Random trades to recover a loss.
❌ Moving SL — Accept the small loss. Don't move it further.
❌ Overtrading — 1-2 good setups per week beats 10 bad trades daily.
❌ Tips — WhatsApp/Telegram tips will lose you money.
❌ Averaging Down — Buying more of a losing stock = doubling mistake.
Winning Habits
✅ Journal Everything — Every trade logged = faster improvement.
✅ Plan Before Entry — Entry, SL, target BEFORE Buy button.
✅ Respect SL — Always. Every time. Small loss beats large loss.
✅ Weekly Review — Review all trades every weekend.
✅ Take Breaks — 3 losses in a row = stop for the day.
✅ Your 2 Setups — Not setup #1 or #2 = not a trade.
